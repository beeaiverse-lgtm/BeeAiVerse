2 Executive Summary
Why BAV is being introduced.

Bee AI Verse is a utility-focused ecosystem built around trading technology, automation, community development, and future digital products. It is built on an existing foundation of TradingView indicators and trading bots offered to a community for approximately three years. BAV, the native ecosystem token, is being introduced to support product access, subscriptions, ecosystem utility, community initiatives, and future projects — with token utility built around real products and services rather than speculation alone.
